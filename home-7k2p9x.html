How the links behave

The bare folder is a dead end on purpose. If a prospect trims a link back to atelioos.github.io/demos/ they get a blank Telaia wall, not a list of everyone else you are pitching. That is why it looked broken — it is doing its job.

Every page is noindexed, so none of them turn up in a search. Anyone with a link can open it, so treat the links themselves as the access control.

Cameron’s demo takes a trade in the URL. Add ?t=mech, ?t=gc or ?t=elec to open it straight into plumbing, general contracting or electrical — useful if you ever want to send a trades-neutral version to somebody who is not him.
