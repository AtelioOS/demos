What the monthly covers

  • Everything in the demonstration. There is no second tier and no module held back.
  • Unlimited seats. The number of people using it does not change what it costs us to run, so it does not change what it costs you.
  • Hosting, backups and updates. Server costs are ours whatever the volume.
  • Support. Written support during business hours, with a named person rather than a queue.
  • Changes. Small changes are part of the subscription. Anything that is a new module is quoted before it is built.

What is not in the figure

Named here so it is never a surprise on an invoice.

  • Advertising spend is never billed through us. If any paid media is ever involved, it goes on your own card, to your own account.
  • Third-party subscriptions you already hold — your storefront, your subscription app, your warehouse, your carriers. We connect to them; we do not resell them.
  • Customs brokerage, accounting and legal fees. We surface the questions. Your advisers answer them and bill you directly.
  • A United States warehouse, if the duty analysis leads you there. That is a third-party cost and we would help you compare it, not mark it up.
05

How this figure was arrived at

It is our standard six-module package rate, not a number written for this conversation. We publish it, and the businesses paying it range from a single restaurant to a manufacturer with three sites.

Firebelly is one company, one catalogue of roughly fifty products, one warehouse and two storefronts. That is genuinely a small build, and it is quoted like one. If discovery shows the scope is larger than the six modules above, we will say so and requote before anything is signed — not after.

This page is a quotation, not an invoice or an agreement. A statement of work and a service agreement follow the discovery call, and both should be read by your lawyer before signature.
